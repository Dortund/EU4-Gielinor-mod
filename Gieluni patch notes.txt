# Beta 1.11.0 Patch Notes (Al Kharid)

# Gielinor Universalis' largest-ever update focuses primarily on the Kharidian Desert with a particular focus on the northern Kharidians, who can form Al Kharid or go an entirely new path!
# There's also improvements to terrain, estates, missionaries, the Runecrafting Crusades, a new Emperor of Kharid mechanic, an Amascutite religion and a whole bunch of extra provinces to play around with.

#######################
# Map
#######################

# Over 50 new provinces, mostly land, representing the OSRS Sailing expansion (RS3's Havenhythe is being left out for the time being)
## Some of these new provinces fall into existing areas; I've tried to adjust mission trees accordingly but I may have missed some.

# Two new types of terrain: Underground and Volcanic.
## Underground terrain is as defensible as mountains, *and* makes forts cheap to maintain on the tile. It's expensive to develop, as you'd expect.
## Volcanic terrain has a reasonable forcelimit, but added attrition. About half of the Wilderness counts as it. It's *very* expensive to develop.
## Underground lava areas (like the TzHaar city) are considered Underground rather than Volcanic.

# Complete rework to sea areas, regions and superregions to be more in line with OSRS Sailing
## Some previously impassable sea provinces in the southwest portion of the map are now navigable

# New province for Keldagrim: Lavaflow, representing the Lava Flow Mine and connected caves
# New province for the Troll Stronghold: Troll Camp, representing the exterior of the Troll Stronghold
# Four additional mainland Kharidian provinces: Shantay Pass, Kalphite Shore, Temple of Isolation and one to represent the old Goebie camp location south of Nardah

# The Empire of Kharid
#######################

# The vanilla game's Mandate of Heaven mechanic is reworked in this mod as the Empire of Kharid! At the start of the game, Menaphos holds the title of Emperor and has a few tributaries in the Kharidian Desert.
# Getting Mandate initially is very difficult, reflective of the difficulty Menaphos had in asserting its authority.
# The Pharoah of Kharid has access to a special covert diplomatic action to create a make-tributary casus belli against any non-boss nation in the Kharidian Desert region.

# The "Expand Settlements" reform is the easiest to unlock and allows you to expand your subject nations' land and generate extra Mandate every 20 years. Under certain conditions it can also generate new nations!
# Through the Karamjan and Wushankoan reforms, you can expand the make-tributary diplomatic action to those respective regions, helping you build a huge tributary empire
# The "Promote the Pantheon" reform lets you propagate Menaphite religions through trade - useful for converting Wushankoan tributaries!
# The Reconstruction of Ullek and Uzer reforms offer good bonuses in their own right, and also spawn their respective countries if the provinces are empty (or buff the province if it's owned/owned by a subject)

#######################
# Global Speed Buff
#######################

# Gielinor Universalis uses a scale that results in large provinces, and consequentially things can feel a bit slower than necessary. As such, I have increased the base speed of the following by approximately 50%:
## Diplomat movement
## Merchant movement
## Infantry speed
## Cavalry speed
## Artillery speed
## Colonist travel time
# I have also given all nations +3 fleet movement as a base bonus; this is approximately +50% to transports/heavies, +75% to galleys, +30% to light ships.
# Given developments in RS3's world map, I may have to entirely rescale the world map in a future patch, which will probably result in a restoration of regular movement speed.

# Religions
#######################

# New religion: Amascutite.
## The Scabarites of Ullek now start following it, and other Menaphite nations can fall into it via the Corruption of Amascut disaster.
## Unlocks access to the Order of the Blood Moon local organisation (costs 100 MIL, +2 manpower in area provinces, +1 local hostile attrition, +1.5% missionary strength, -10% culture-conversion cost)
## Unlocks a special covert action to spread corruption in Menaphite countries, but gives them a casus belli against you.
## Anyone with the religion is forced into the Thrall of Amascut T2 government reform with a unique Enthrallment mechanic.
### High Enthrallment allows you to coastal raid, effectively raze provinces, spy with ease and get some nice modifiers - but you'll have a hard time making friends and will generate a *lot* of aggressive expansion.
### Getting your Enthrallment below 10% is the only way to escape the faith aside from being force-converted through war (religious rebels will only lower your Enthrallment rating and not convert you!)

# General
## With the exception of the Hedonistic religious group, *all religions* now get access to decisions to boost their missionary strength.
### Saradomin, Zamorak and Guthix get the most missionary strength, followed by nations featured in Armies of Gielinor or the God Wars Dungeon.
## Fixed some issues with religious propogation via trade. A nation following Saradomin, Zamorak or Guthix can propogate religion using trade in provinces that follow the Wushanko Guardians.
## Fixed the Religious Aspects alert to match your current religion
## More religion-specific modifiers are correctly removed when your religion is changed.
## Changing religion to Godless, Guthix or V should correctly trigger the event to choose religious school.

# Queen of Ashes
## Special event chain to unlock the religion - chance of happening if you have at least 12 recently-razed provinces in your lands. Don't try following this event chain while following Amascut; it can't end well.
## Added Festival mechanic (requires Origins expansion). This is a modified version of the vanilla game's Jewish mechanics but with some notable distinctions.
### The three categories of religious aspects are Fuel, Treatment and Dance.
### Fuel aspects determine what you get out of Festivals, and are doubled in power if you have a trading bonus in one of the relevant trade goods
### Treatment aspects represent additives to fires to make them stronger, inspired by Gnome Firelighters, Phoenixes and Troll Salts. Holding relevant provinces will add extra bonuses.
### Dance aspects affect your prowess in warfare and allow you to generate extra faith power.
