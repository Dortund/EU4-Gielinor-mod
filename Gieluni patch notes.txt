##Gielinor Universalis Patch Notes

# Beta 1.10.0 Patch Notes (Varlamore)

This is really a combination of an unfinished patch I was working on some time ago and some recent fixes, but given the net result mostly involved Varlamore I thought it'd make sense to name the patch after it.

Although there are some map adjustments in line with the book The Gift of Guthix, I haven't implemented the Fremennik clans mentioned in the book yet as I wanted to get the patch out sooner.
There's also some map adjustments in line with OSRS' Sailing, though islands will come at a later date.

#######################
# Map
#######################

# Renamed >160 sea (and some land) provinces in accordance with upcoming OSRS Sailing
## Full Sailing changes are held off for the time being as the Sailing maps are not finalised (and have some overlap with Wushanko)

# Adjusted Kharidian coast in line with the OSRS map.
## Tempoross Cove is now considered part of Menaphos trade node instead of South Lum due to its relocation, and has been moved to where it'll be post-Sailing.
## Menaphos' wider area has the same number of provinces as before, but some are no longer islands.
## Akthanakos Island shrunk to match the size of the equivalent island for OSRS Sailing

# Renamed a couple of provinces on Tutorial Island to lessen the meta aspects of the island, and incorporate some very obscure mentions of Jarn
# Lumbridge Swamp is now renamed by Misthalin dynamically rather than by events

#######################
# National Flavour
#######################

# New Boss Nation and Province: Tapoyauik. Found in northeastern Varlamore.
# New Nation: Twilight Emissaries. Appears on later starts.
# Try culture-converting in Locus Oasis...
# Tweaked uses of "global regiment cost", "infantry cost", "cavalry cost" and "artillery cost" in national ideas for balancing reasons
# Replaced culture-conversion cost for time in national ideas of Hallowvale, Iban, Idol Depths, Morytania, Nightmare, Strangler, Sea Slugs

# Four new tribes of Asgarnia described in The Gift of Guthix replace existing noncanonical nations:
## Caracelli - Replaces Cynling.
## Kareivis - Replaces Singrene.
## Massama - Replaces Feorweg.
## Narvra - Replaces Brerdas.
# Also adjusted some Asgarnian colours to be more alike each other to give the region more of a sense of coherence

# Great Kourend
## Added Royal Accord of Twill event which can fire in the aftermath of Varlamore's War of Betrayal disaster

# Varlamore
## Updated flag
## New event chain reflecting Imafore's Betrayal. Must be in the Age of Adventurers following Ralos or Ranul, Kourend must exist and be a monarchy.
## New Disaster: War of Betrayal. Caused via the Imafore's Betrayal event chain *or* by declaring war on a nation of Kourendite or Lovakengj culture.
### Activating this disaster before the Age of Adventurers makes it take longer and have bigger penalties.
### Depending on the outcome you are granted access to the Reformed Shining Kingdom or Tarnished Kingdom governments, both variations on the classic Shining Kingdom.
## New event to spawn the Twilight Emissaries

#######################
# Religions
#######################

# Guthix - New religious school: Proactive. Used by Kareivis, Narvra and the Void Knights.

# V - Vampyric syncretism gives culture-conversion time reduction instead of cost

#######################
# Cultures
#######################

# Cam Torum is now considered its own culture in the Dwarven group, separate from Imcando.

# Rennard is now the primary nation of the Dimnes culture instead of Dark Wizards. Hopefully this should create more logical rebels.

#######################
# Government
#######################

# "Quidamortem Boss Nation" renamed to "Quidamortem Government" with fewer restrictions than before, but also fewer bonuses. The subsequent government reforms they may choose have also been adjusted.
# Boss, Island Boss and Quidamortem governments have nerfed intrinsic bonuses, but now has access to the Boss Endurance mechanic.
## Boss Endurance ticks up slowly over time making boss nations harder to defeat as time goes on - but they also become richer if you feel like fighting them for cash.
## War exhaustion causes nations to lose Boss Endurance.
# Government reforms for Boss Nations have also been reined in power-wise to account for the Boss Endurance mechanic.

# Vampyre Kingdom - Blood Power now gives culture conversion time reduction instead of cost reduction

# I attempted to implement a new system of Dwarven Colonies but it caused so much trouble I cut it, with some stuff in the code but commented out or inaccessible. I might reimplement the idea in future though.

#######################
# Events
#######################

# New set of Wilderness Border events concerning outlaws. The mechanics of these events are a stealth means to bring institution spread into the Wilderness as you can choose to exile the outlaws.

#######################
# Institutions
#######################

# Runecrafting institution now spreads slower by default, but faster than before in provinces with Asgarnian, Fremennik, Kandarin, Kharidian, Kourendite, Misthalanian, Varlamorean, Cam Torum, Imcando or Lovakengj culture.
## This is intended to reflect the rapid spread of Runecrafting through primarily human realms. The Imcando and Cam Torum dwarves were also rapid adopters, and Lovakengj is included due to being a related population.
# Runecrafting institution spread for Runes and latent Runes adjusted; latent Runes are still slower to gain the institution than non-latent ones, but the gap is closed. This is to help main-continent human realms.

#######################
# Monuments
#######################

# New Monument: Temple of Twilight. Found in northeastern Varlamore, requires the Ranul or Ralos faith. Offers a fixed boost to land forcelimit and bonuses to manpower in true-faith provinces.

# Chambers of Xeric offers less regiment cost reduction until tier 3
# Forge of Giants no longer offers reduced army maintenance costs, but instead offers extra goods produced for the province
# Fortis Aqueduct has been adjusted to offer region-wide bonuses
# Tuai Leit Library now offers direct institution growth in its province instead of just a growth multiplier
